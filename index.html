Capacity ≠ cash unless spend actually drops. Released time is potential capacity. Validate adoption, time, quality, and actual expenditure before claiming savings.

See the math & assumptions +

Weekly hours = tasks per week × max(0, minutes before − minutes after) ÷ 60.

Annual capacity value = weekly hours × hourly rate × 52.

First-year net = annual capacity value − annual tool cost − implementation cost.

Payback months = implementation cost ÷ ((annual capacity value − annual tool cost) ÷ 12). There is no payback when monthly net capacity value is zero or negative. Slower workflows release zero capacity in this model; any extra time cost must be assessed separately.

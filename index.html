Calculation method and validation plan

Weekly capacity = tasks × (current minutes − minutes after change) ÷ 60. Annual labor capacity value = weekly capacity × hourly labor cost × 52. First-year modeled net value subtracts annual recurring tool cost and one-time implementation cost. Capacity-value payback divides implementation cost by monthly labor capacity value less recurring monthly cost; no positive monthly value means no modeled payback.

Validate with a representative timed baseline and pilot. Check accuracy, exception rate, rework, adoption, and total operating cost. Staff time released is capacity, and becomes cash savings only when there is an actual reduction in spend. The sample assumes 52 working weeks and full task coverage; adjust volume to reflect holidays, adoption, and seasonality. The model excludes any cost not entered above.
